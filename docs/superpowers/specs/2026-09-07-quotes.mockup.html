What this is

A price agreed, not listed

A provider marks a service "por orçamento" and today the customer can only send a message. These screens close the loop: the customer asks, the provider answers with a price, a date and a duration, the customer accepts, and a booking is born already waiting for M-Pesa. Same visual direction as the listings and inbox refresh: white page, Figtree, navy for what matters, one blue button per page, hairlines instead of boxes.

REQUESTED ── provider proposes ──▶ PROPOSED ── customer accepts ──▶ ACCEPTED ──▶ booking, PENDING_PAYMENT ──▶ M-Pesa ──▶ CONFIRMED
   ├ provider declines ▶ DECLINED         ├ provider revises ▶ PROPOSED (previous one superseded)
   ├ customer withdraws ▶ WITHDRAWN       ├ provider withdraws ▶ DECLINED      ├ customer declines ▶ REJECTED
   └ 48 h pass ▶ EXPIRED                  └ 72 h pass ▶ EXPIRED

  • The proposal is the hero

    On the customer's detail page the price is set at 40px in navy with the date and duration under it, like a tile price grown up. Nothing else on the page competes with it.

  • Every step can carry a note and files

    The request, each proposal and every refusal take free text and up to five attachments. One attachment table, one upload flow, the contact detector on all three texts.

  • The provider sees enough to price, not enough to leave

    Description, photos, deadline, bairro and city, first name. The exact street, the phone and the email arrive on the booking once it is confirmed, by the rule the bookings already keep.

The split updates as the provider types. "Recebe 8 820 MZN" is the largest number in the form on purpose: it is the one the provider is deciding on.

"Está livre na sua agenda" is a real check against the member's bookings before sending; a clash is refused here, not discovered by the customer at acceptance.

The decline dialog is the same shape for the customer's "Recusar": a short list of reasons, an optional note, optional files. The reason is a token so the other side reads it in their language; the note is free text and passes the contact detector.

After sending, this page shows the proposal as the customer sees it (the type block from plate 4), "válida até", and "Rever proposta" as text. The form stays one click away until the customer decides.

8 · Seven states, two readers

What each state says on each side

Same status, different sentence, because the two people are asking different questions. The dot colour follows whose turn it is, not the state's name.

StatusThe customer readsThe provider reads
REQUESTEDÀ espera de propostaPor responder
PROPOSEDProposta recebidaProposta enviada
ACCEPTEDAceite · ver reservaAceite · ver reserva
DECLINEDRecusado pelo prestadorRecusou
REJECTEDRecusou a propostaCliente recusou
WITHDRAWNRetirou o pedidoCliente retirou
EXPIREDExpirado · o prestador não respondeu / a proposta caducouExpirado · não respondeu / o cliente não decidiu

Amber is "the other side owes you"; navy is "your move". A customer looking at REQUESTED is waiting (amber); a provider looking at the same row is owed (navy). Green, red and grey are terminal and read the same way everywhere.

EXPIRED carries its cause in the clock line, so "expirado" never leaves someone guessing who dropped it.
